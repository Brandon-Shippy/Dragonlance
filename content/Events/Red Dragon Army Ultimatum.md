**Type:** Event **Date:** Session 2 **Location:** [[Vogler]]

## What We Know

- A messenger in full scale armor arrived from the North
- She presented a parchment with an official ultimatum
- Message was from [[Belephaion]], "The Voice of [[Takhisis]]"
- Demanded [[Vogler]] quarter the soldiers of the [[Red Dragon Army]] or face death

## The Message

> "People of Vogler, by the orders of [[Belephaion]], The Voice of [[Takhisis]]: You will quarter the soldiers of the invincible [[Red Dragon Army]] tonight. Refuse and die."

## Context

- Delivered after party discovered thousands of soldiers in [[West Forest]]
- Confirmed by both party scouts and [[Jaev]]'s independent scouting
- [[Takhisis]] revealed as the Queen of Dragons, greatest of all evil gods
- Messenger bore spiral symbol insignia

## Response

- [[Vogler]] chose not to surrender
- Immediate evacuation ordered
- [[Kudgel]] and [[Becklin]] staying behind to buy time
- Refugees fleeing to [[Kalaman]] via southern river route

## Significance

- First direct communication from [[Red Dragon Army]]
- Revealed [[Takhisis]]'s involvement
- Forced mass evacuation
- Set deadline for compliance (that night)

## Participants

- [[Belephaion]] - Sender of ultimatum
- [[Takhisis]] - Divine authority behind the army
- [[Red Dragon Army]] - Military force demanding quarters
- Messenger - Delivered the ultimatum in full scale armor
- [[Vogler]] leadership - Received ultimatum

## Aftermath

- [[Evacuation of Vogler]] immediately began
- Party mission shifted to clearing escape route
- [[Becklin]] entrusted mysterious box to [[Derret]]
- Expected casualties: [[Kudgel]] and [[Becklin]]

## Mysteries

- What happens if [[Vogler]] refuses? (Mass attack expected)
- How much time before the army attacks?
- Is the messenger from [[Red Dragon Army]] still in town?
- What is [[Belephaion]]'s true power?
