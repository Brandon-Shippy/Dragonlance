**Type:** Settlement (Fishing Town)

### What We Know

- Fishing town on a murky river
- Population: Humans, [[Kenders]], [[Hill Dwarves]]
- Has incomplete stone bridge from before [[The Cataclysm]]
- Dozens of small boats on the river
- Soldier encampment to the north
- Never conquered by [[Isthar Empire]] thanks to [[Knights of Solamnia]]
- Site of annual [[King Fisher Festival]]
- Most of the militia died in [[The Ambush]]
- **UNDER THREAT** - [[Red Dragon Army]] demanded surrender
- **EVACUATING** - refugees fleeing south via river to [[Kalaman]]

### Leadership

- [[Mayor Raven Uthvoughler]] (suspicious)

### Landmarks

- [[Thornwall Keep]] - by the gate
- [[Brasscrab]] - large circular building next to port (interrogation site)
- [[Mayor's Building]] - northern city circle
- [[The Fishbowl]] - restaurant famous for fish pie
- [[Fisher's Market]] - runs down middle of town
- U-shaped house - very rich country house
- Incomplete stone bridge (pre-[[The Cataclysm]])

### History

- Site of [[Battle of High Hill]]
- Defended by [[Knights of Solamnia]] from [[Isthar Empire]]
- Named after [[Lord Deckter Vogler]]

### Current Crisis

- Militia decimated after [[The Ambush]]
- Under threat from [[Red Dragon Army]]
- Mass evacuation in progress
- [[Kudgel]] and [[Becklin]] staying behind to buy time
- Southern cliffside route being cleared for evacuation

### Evacuation Route

- Southern river route
- Leads to backside of cliffs
- Heads east to [[Kalaman]]
- Party clearing cliffside
