## **Session Summary**

### **Act 1: Aftermath of The Ambush**

[[Mayor Raven Uthvoughler]] thanks the party for helping prevent the ambush on [[Vogler]]. Despite her thanks, the party remains suspicious of her involvement.

[[Silus]] (me) cleaned off [[Thalia]]'s blade for her - discovered it has "**The Oath**" inscribed on it.

### **Act 2: Interrogation at the Brasscrab**

Party captured a surviving [[Iron Clad Regime]] member. [[Kudgel]] has her under control.

[[Becklin]] is interrogating the survivor at the [[Brasscrab]]. The party goes in to assist with the interrogation.

**Key Information Learned:**

- [[Jaev]], [[Kudgel]]'s lieutenant, was in the outer camp with the other 50 [[Iron Clad Regime]] members who did NOT take part in the reenactment
- [[Grigonis]] (an ogre) was the leader of the [[Iron Clad Regime]] faction we killed in [[The Ambush]]
- The 50 regime members in the outer camp were likely NOT involved in the betrayal
- [[Haive]] and [[Silus]] double-teamed the interrogation

**Prisoner's Testimony:**

- [[Grigonis]] planned to kill [[Kudgel]] and take control of the entire [[Iron Clad Regime]]
- [[Grigonis]] went into the [[West Forest]] and came back with a bunch of gold
- He used the gold to hire mercenaries and planned to raid [[Vogler]] afterward
- **The man who gave [[Grigonis]] his money wore black and red armor with a hood**

### **Act 3: Thalia's Confession**

[[Thalia]] walked in and saw the interrogation scene. [[Silus]] left the room to talk with her privately.

**Important Revelation:**

- [[Thalia]] believes the prisoner does not deserve to die
- **[[Thalia]] cannot control her powers** - she revealed this to [[Silus]]

Hearing this, [[Silus]] rushes back to try and stop the execution.

### **Act 4: Mercy and Unity**

[[Haive]] holds off on killing the prisoner because he couldn't fight back - showed mercy.

[[Zagreus]] turns away because he realized the prisoner was scared and this likely wasn't done by his own volition.

**Forming the Party:** The group gathers to discuss staying together for the foreseeable future.

- They agree to join together on [[Haive]]'s condition: **everyone carries their own weight**
- [[Silus]] tells the party to not go overboard

[[Kudgel]] tells the party to investigate the [[West Forest]].

### **Act 5: Dawn Investigation**

Party plans to head out at 5am to investigate the forest.

Woke up at 5am. [[Silus]] made everyone breakfast. [[Silus]] appears pale - [[Haive]] notices and urges him to eat.

**On the way to the forest:**

- Found footprints older than a day
- **Two types:** boot prints and reptilian prints
- Pattern suggests patrol routes

### **Act 6: The Army in the Valley**

The forest opens up to a shallow valley visible from a ledge.

**CRITICAL DISCOVERY:**

- Hundreds of campfires in the valley
- **Thousands of soldiers** controlling the shallow valley
- This is a **strong, organized army**

Party immediately returns to warn [[Becklin]].

### **Act 7: Confirmation and the Messenger**

[[Jaev]], [[Kudgel]]'s lieutenant, had independently scouted and saw the same camp - confirms the party's report.

**The Messenger Arrives:** A messenger in full scale armor arrives from the North.

She presents a parchment with a dire message:

> **"People of Vogler, by the orders of [[Belephaion]], The Voice of [[Takhisis]]: You will quarter the soldiers of the invincible [[Red Dragon Army]] tonight. Refuse and die."**

**Critical Information:**

- [[Takhisis]] is one of the Gods of [[Krynn]]
- She is the **greatest of all evil Gods**
- Also known as the **Queen of Dragons**
- The messenger bears a **spiral symbol insignia**

### **Act 8: Evacuation Plans**

The party plans to evacuate [[Vogler]] through the southern river route, heading east to [[Kalaman]].

**[[Becklin]]'s Request:**

- [[Becklin]] has a large wooden box in [[Thornwall Keep]]
- Asks party to fetch it and give it to [[Derret]]

**Evacuation Route:**

- Southern river leads to the backside of the cliffs where the scouts are
- [[Silus]] tells the party to go South and clean out the cliffside
- [[Silus]] goes alone to fetch the box

**The Mysterious Box:**

- Heavy, about half [[Silus]]'s size
- [[Silus]] can barely carry it
- Contains **clanking metal** inside
- Contents unknown

### **Act 9: The Sacrifice**

**[[Kudgel]] and [[Becklin]] are staying behind** - they cannot evacuate in time.

The party will evacuate with the villagers to help them escape to [[Kalaman]].
