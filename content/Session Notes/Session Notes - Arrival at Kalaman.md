## Session Summary

### Act 1: Arrival at Kalaman

Party arrives at [[Kalaman]] by boat, seeing big towers as they approach.

**The Refugee Crisis:**

- **100 refugee camps** outside the city on the shore
- Camps filled with refugees from **different places** (not just [[Vogler]])
- City is being overwhelmed by the refugee influx
- Making camp outside city walls

### Act 2: The Ancient Kender

**[[Haive]]'s Divine Encounter:**

- [[Haive]] helped an old [[Kenders|kender]] man with a sprained ankle
- When [[Haive]] healed the ankle, **the old man's head snapped in the other direction**
- **[[Haive]] was transported into another dimension** - spiritual/deity realm
- **Deity revealed:** This kender **predates [[The Cataclysm]]** (impossibly old)
- **[[Haive]] received a blessing** from his deity

### Act 3: The Gate

**City Entry Problems:**

- Guards at the gate **turning away certain races** (discrimination)
- Guards have **no specific insignia** (unusual - whose authority?)
- Long customs line along city walls outside
- Tattered merchant stalls outside walls
- Priest preaching to everyone in line

### Act 4: Silus Tries to Skip the Line

**Failed Attempt:**

- [[Silus]] went to front of line trying to get in early
- Guard stopped the party
- [[Silus]] claimed business with [[Mages of High Sorcery]]
- **Guard didn't know what that was** - forced party back in line

**The Supervisor:**

- Guard supervisor ([[Sodin]]) being a hardass
- **[[Baccahrus]] family entered ahead of time** (privilege/influence)
- Supervisor eventually lets party in to **represent [[Vogler]]**
- Party given audience to make case for refugee shelter

### Act 5: Entering Kalaman

**[[Sodin]]'s Decision:**

- [[Sodin]] lets party in (seems disinterested)
- Party enters [[Kalaman]]

**The Forges:**

- **[[Kadmos Forge]]** - on the left
    - Short door (presumably dwarven forge)
- **[[Tyria Forge]]** - on the right
    - Sharper, less smoke
    - Sounds of hot steel

### Act 6: Tyria's Magic Shop

**[[Silus]] Visits [[Tyria Forge]]:**

- Owner is a dwarf, better kempt than typical
- **She has a talisman from [[Mages of High Sorcery]]**
- Opens **secret door in the back**
- Runs a **hidden magic shop** behind the forge
- Another sweaty dwarf bursts into the shop

### Act 7: Preparing the Case

**Mission:**

- Party heading to castle to argue why [[Vogler]] refugees should be housed
- Plan: **Leverage information about [[Red Dragon Army]]** to secure entry

### Act 8: Baccahrus Confrontation

**Outside the Hall:**

- [[Baccahrus the Young]] tries to block party entrance
- His father inside speaking for the people
- **[[Silus]] uses mage hand to unbuckle [[Baccahrus the Young|Baccahrus's]] pants in public** as they round the corner
- Public humiliation

### Act 9: Audience with the Governor

**Meeting [[Governor Kaeloph Meit]]:**

- Governor welcomes party
- [[Baccahrus the Elder]] present (father)
- Party convinces [[Governor Kaeloph Meit|Meit]] that [[Baccahrus the Elder]] is NOT the representative
- [[Baccahrus the Elder]] gets sent out

**The Council Meeting:**

- Party sent away while councillors deliberate
- **DECISION: Refugees allowed inside walls**
- **Given food and shelter**

- Party must share **ALL information about [[Red Dragon Army]]**

- Party offered positions as **informants**
- Will receive **pay**

**Who Accepted:**

- [[Silus]]
- [[Haive]]
- [[Vaubin]]
- [[Derret]]
- ([[Thalia]], [[Calendur]], [[Zagreus]] status unclear)

**Payment/Symbol:**
