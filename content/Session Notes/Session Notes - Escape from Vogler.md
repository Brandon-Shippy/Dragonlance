## Session Summary

### Act 1: Southern Port Preparations

Party gathered at the Southern Port with [[Becklin's Mysterious Box]].

**The Weber Family Crisis:**

- Weber family missing from evacuation
- Family of four: two parents, two kids
- Bureaucrat at port asking annoying questions about party size to account for escape routes
- [[Silus]] leaves [[Vaubin]] to handle him

**Division of Labor:**

- [[Thalia]]: Getting rope and tying boats together
- [[Haive]]: Helping pack boats
- [[Zagreus]]: Calming children down
- [[Silus]] and [[Calendur]]: Going to Weber home to retrieve the family

### Act 2: The Weber Home

[[Silus]] and [[Calendur]] went to the Weber home to convince them to evacuate.

**The Confrontation:**

- Weber father refused to leave
- [[Calendur]] convinced them (didn't go well but worked)
- [[Calendur]] gave his name
- **[[Silus]] did NOT give his name** (maintaining anonymity?)

### Act 3: Raiding the Baccahrus Estate

[[Silus]], [[Thalia]], and [[Calendur]] raided the [[Baccahrus]] family estate.

**Loot Acquired:**

- **6 rings**, each engraved with the [[Baccahrus]] family crest
- Presumably valuable/significant

### Act 4: Final Words with the Defenders

Party went to the camp to speak with [[Becklin]] and [[Kudgel]].

**The Last Stand:**

- [[Becklin]] and [[Kudgel]] going to front of battlefield to hold the line
- Party stays back to help with evacuation
- This is goodbye

### Act 5: The Red Dragon Army Attacks

**The Enemy Arrives:**

- [[Red Dragon Army]] revealed to be **dragon people** (draconians?)
- They swooped in attacking during evacuation
- Party defends civilians while they escape

**The False Dragon:**

- A dragon flew down from the Northern gate
- Breathing fire
- **IT'S A FAKE DRAGON** - actually a mechanical construct
- Dragon people are manning/piloting it
- Elaborate war machine designed to terrify

### Act 6: Derret's Stand

**[[Derret]]'s Moment:**

- [[Derret]] put up a good fight
- Battled what appeared to be the head dragon/dragon leader
- Proving himself in combat

### Act 7: Fighting Retreat

- Party defeated enemies in front of them
- **The human section of [[Red Dragon Army]] just now marching in**
- Two-pronged attack: dragon people first, then human soldiers
- Most of village managed to escape
- Party makes it to the final boat

### Act 8: Waiting for Jaev

- Party held the boat for [[Jaev]]
- **[[Jaev]] is [[Becklin]]'s Lieutenant** (not [[Kudgel]]'s as previously thought)
- [[Jaev]] made it to the boat

### Act 9: The Box Revealed

**[[Becklin's Mysterious Box]] Contents:**

- [[Silus]] gave [[Derret]] the box
- Inside: **A full suit of armor**
- Armor signifies [[Derret]] is now a **[[Knights of Solamnia|Knight of Solamnia]], [[Order of the Sword]]**
- [[Becklin]]'s final gift to her student - his knighthood

### Act 10: Journey to Kalaman

- Party now headed to [[Kalaman]]
- Civilians safe on boats
- [[Vogler]] left behind
- [[Becklin]] and [[Kudgel]]'s fate unknown
