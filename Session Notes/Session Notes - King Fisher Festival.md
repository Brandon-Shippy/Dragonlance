## **Session Summary**

### **Act 1: The Barb - Night of the Eye**

Started at [[Brushbook Tavern]] during the [[Triple Eclipse]]. A bard played lute while we received chalky grey talismans marking us for the meeting at [[The Barb]].

Traveled to [[The Barb]], a giant spire on the mountain range - ancient ruins predating [[The Cataclysm]]. It's known as a meeting place for the [[Mages of High Sorcery]]. We arrived on the [[Night of the Eye]], when [[Krynn]]'s three moons align and magic flares.

Inside, scary torches lit our way to a room with tapestries depicting the three moons. A lady stood in the middle conversing with another participant. Portal doors opened to another rotunda with elaborate runes carved around a single pedestal holding an ornate key.

**The Test**: I traced the important runes - they formed a knock spell. Made a scroll of it. We passed the test and were given a scroll with instructions to find [[Wyhan]], a black robed mage in [[Kalaman]]. [[Thalia]]'s eyes glowed during this.

### **Act 2: Arrival at Vogler**

Traveled to [[Vogler]], a fishing town with an incomplete stone bridge dating to before [[The Cataclysm]]. The river has dozens of small boats on murky water.

**Met the Party at the Docks:**

- [[Calendur]] - guy with broken wrist watch, hastily put together mismatched suit
- [[Zagreus]] - guy in full armor and fur cape, Dark Souls style, from southern [[Ergoth]]
- [[Vaubin]] - bigger guy with fur armor and spiked pauldrons, barbarian
- [[Haive]] - Minotaur! Not groomed, dreads with loud leaves and twigs

Explored the town - population of Humans, [[Kenders]], and [[Hill Dwarves]]. There's a soldier encampment to the north. [[Zagreus]] mentioned [[Becklin]] like an uncle figure for him.

**Town Landmarks:**

- [[Brasscrab]] - big circle building next to port
- [[Mayor's Building]] - city circle building to the north
- [[The Fishbowl]] - to the right of mayor's building (great fish pie)
- [[Fisher's Market]] - straight down the middle
- U-shaped house - very rich country house

We went to [[Fisher's Market]]. Most fishermen were out getting boats ready, boat inspectors around. There's a sign for good luck at docks with a king fisher bird. Saw an older [[Kenders|Kender]] smoking.

Traded a cigarette with the fish puppet lady. [[Haive]] traded a tiger tooth for a fish puppet marionette. Guy with mask (didn't eat fish, gave to friend - too badass for food).
