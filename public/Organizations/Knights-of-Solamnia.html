The Ambush

Type: Event/Battle

What We Know

  • The reenactment of Battle of High Hill became a real, deadly attack
  • Setup: Town militia on hill, Iron Clad Regime at bottom
  • Party fought on the side of Vogler militia
  • Most of Vogler militia was killed
  • Even Iron Clad Regime commander (Couldren) seemed unaware it was planned
  • Mayor Raven Uthvoughler made suspicious “happy coincidence” comment about Iron Clad Regime’s presence

Combat Events

  • Party killed all attackers
  • Zagreus went berserk (described as “lowkey freaky”)
  • Calendur snapped an enemy’s head off
  • Mysterious Crystals emerged from the decapitated enemy

Casualties

  • Most of Vogler militia dead
  • Unknown enemy casualties

Suspects

  • Mayor Raven Uthvoughler (highly suspicious)
  • Iron Clad Regime (possibly unwitting participants)

Mysteries

  • Who planned the real attack?
  • Why betray the militia?
  • What are the Mysterious Crystals?
